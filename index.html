The whole point?

Slackademics is a hilarious social deduction card game where players must ‘work together’ to complete university group assignments. The player who spent the most effort partying and the least time contributing to the group assignment will win the game… provided they don’t get caught and expelled!

The group evaluation

Social Deduction. Who is the Slacker?

Each round, during the dreaded group evaluation, players must use their social deduction skills to work out who the Slacker is. The Slacker is whoever spent the highest effort partying and gave the lowest contribution to the group project. If the Slacker is discovered by the group vote, they'll face additional consequences, and can possibly be kicked out of the group entirely!
